What aloud is

aloud is a voice-based meditation facilitator. You speak; your speech is transcribed; a language model generates gentle facilitation; and the response is spoken back to you. To do this, aloud needs three kinds of AI service: speech-to-text, a language model, and text-to-speech. These terms cover both ways of reaching them.

Two ways to use aloud

aloud is designed to give you a choice about where your data goes:

  • Your own way (local / bring-your-own). On platforms that allow it (such as the desktop app), aloud runs on your device and connects directly to the providers you configure with your own credentials, or runs fully on-device. aloud has no server in the middle and receives nothing. See our privacy policy for the details.
  • aloud cloud. Our optional hosted service for situations where running your own isn't practical, such as the web app and, in the future, mobile apps. You sign in, optionally buy credits, and aloud cloud relays your audio and text to third-party AI and voice providers on your behalf. We don't keep your session content.

Wherever your platform physically allows it, you keep the ability to run your own models and bring your own keys; using aloud cloud is always a convenience, never a requirement.
