Delete your account

Last updated: July 21, 2026

This page is for aloud cloud accounts, the optional sign-in used for hosted AI and credits. If you run aloud locally with your own providers, there is no account to delete; everything already lives on your device.

From the app

  1. Open Account (from the menu).
  2. Choose Delete account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate. The same screen exists in the mobile and web apps.

From a browser, without the app

  1. Go to aloud.rest/app and sign in the way you usually do (Google, Apple, or email).
  2. Open Account → Delete account.

Can't sign in, or prefer not to? Email lexkrusz@gmail.com from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in identities (Google, Apple, or email + password) are removed and your email address is scrubbed from our records, along with your signup IP. If you'd opted in to email updates, that preference goes with the address, so we can't email you again.
  • Any remaining credit balance is forfeited, so spend it first if you can.
  • What remains is an anonymous credit ledger (purchases and metered usage no longer tied to you), which we keep for accounting. Data scrubbed from the live database ages out of backups within about a day.
  • Your session transcripts were never in the cloud; they live on your device. Delete them from the app's history, or by uninstalling.

More detail on what we do and don't hold is in the privacy policy.
