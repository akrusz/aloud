Four months of building, told through the commits

A voice meditation app that began as a script in a terminal and grew, over 866 commits, into something you can run on your own machine or open in a browser. The throughline was access: keep every way of running it on the table, and let each person choose what fits their privacy, convenience, and cost.

The idea underneath all of it

Meditation tools ask you to trust them with something private. aloud. was built so you never have to trade that trust for convenience. Run it fully offline with local models, bring your own API keys, or use the hosted version when you just want it to work. Same practice, your call on the dials.

Genesis, in a terminal
Meditation PalCLI

The whole pipeline arrives in a single day under its first name, Meditation Pal: microphone to Whisper to a language model and back out through text to speech. Voice detection, turn taking, silence handling, and gentle check ins are there from the first commit. Inspired by Jhourney's somatic exploration technique. Several LLM providers and a couple of TTS engines follow within hours.

The push to a real desktop product
glooownoting modev0.9.1 → v0.10.0

The busiest stretch of the whole project. glooow becomes something people can install and update: native Mac and Windows installers, an auto updater, an about page, OS native config, a settings page, an interactive onboarding wizard, and a session start tour. Noting mode arrives as a second way to practice. The first tagged release ships Mar 22, then versions land almost daily up to v0.10.0 by month end.

April
A quiet month
glooowsilent practicev0.10.1 → v0.11.3

Things slow right down. This stretch lined up with a period when my own nervous system was more sensitive, and I turned toward quieter, more silent practices to understand what was going on. The code reflects it: only light consolidation, like better Ollama handling, tuning the voice detector to ignore sounds that are not speech, and documentation to sort out the terms of service question around subscription routing. The lull was part of the practice, not a gap in it.

Energy comes back hard. The codebase gets ported to TypeScript with a clean provider abstraction covering OpenAI, OpenRouter, Venice, and Groq. On May 18 the second rename happens: glooow becomes aloud. The next day, v1.0.0 ships.

Late May – June
A hosted option joins the local one
aloud.cloud + billingongoing

The hosted version arrives and sits alongside everything that came before. A backend with a credit ledger denominated in clouds, Stripe checkout, giftable credits, retreat passes, usage metering, sign in with Apple, email, and Google, plus admin tooling and database backups. None of it retires the local app. It widens the menu so the spectrum is complete, from fully private and free to run, to fully managed and pay as you go.
