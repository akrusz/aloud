## Full description

aloud is an app that lets you use your voice to explore your inner world and experience deeper meditation. aloud has three modes: exploration, noting, and felt-sense.

exploration is a dyadic format where you speak in realtime about what you're noticing in your body and emotions, and the facilitator asks gentle questions to help you go deeper. you can also choose to focus on your internal parts; people who find IFS or other parts-work useful will like this too. this mode can be useful for learning meditative states like jhana.

noting is a simpler mode where you speak just one or two words that describe one thing you feel in that moment - such as warmth, tingling, or belly tension. you can set up sound effects, spoken phrases, or simulated participants to help you keep your ideal rhythm between quiet rest and active spoken noticing.

felt-sense mode is inspired by Gendlin's Focusing. this is a six-step method that walks you through the process of settling in, finding something that's bubbling up from inside you, allowing it to resonate, and receiving what comes up from your unconscious.

any mode can be used with a set length. the optional meditation timer lets the facilitator draw smoothly toward a close near the end and tells you vocally when your time is up.

aloud uses text-to-speech, speech-to-text, and AI/LLM brains for some modes; you have full choice of providers. aloud offers the "aloud cloud" that connects you to curated providers without any additional setup. this costs us money to run, so it uses credits; you get some credits free just for connecting a Google or Apple account. Or, if you have keys for OpenRouter or other providers, you can use those instead.
