## Full description

With aloud, you meditate by talking. It listens and talks back. There are three modes: Exploration, Noting, and Felt Sense.

Exploration is a conversation: you say what you're noticing in your body and emotions, and the facilitator asks short questions back. You can also focus on your internal parts; people who find IFS or other parts work useful will like this too. It can also help with learning meditative states like jhana.

Noting is simpler: you say one or two words for one thing you feel in the moment, such as warmth, tingling, or belly tension. Sound effects, spoken phrases, or simulated participants can help you keep a rhythm between quiet rest and spoken noticing.

Felt Sense mode is inspired by Gendlin's Focusing: six steps that walk you through settling in, sensing how something on your mind feels in your body, finding words for it, checking that they fit, asking it questions, and receiving whatever comes up.

Any mode can have a set length. With the optional timer, the facilitator winds down near the end and says when time is up.

You can keep your eyes closed the whole time. Just say "set a timer for ten minutes", "talk slower", "hide the clock" or "end the session", in your own words, even two at once.

Text-to-speech, speech-to-text, and an AI/LLM power some modes; you have full choice of providers. aloud cloud connects you to the providers we picked, with no extra setup. It costs us money to run, so it uses credits; you get some free just for signing in with Google. Or, if you have keys for OpenRouter or other providers, you can use those instead.

### What to Test

This is the first iPhone beta of aloud, which already runs on the web and desktop. What I most want to hear about is the audio on a real phone: whether it hears you, and how its voice sounds.

- Start a Noting session (no account needed). Does it hear you? Is its voice at a sensible volume?
- Sign in with Apple (it comes with free credits), then try Exploration for a few minutes.
- Lock the phone for 30 seconds mid-session, then unlock. Does the session pick up again, at the same volume?
- Try it once with headphones, Bluetooth if you have them.
- Say "set a timer for five minutes" or "end the session".
- On an iPad? All of the above, and how the layout sits when you turn it or resize the window.

### Beta App Review notes

Tick "Sign-in required" and give the demo account.

This is a voice-only meditation app: you speak, it listens and answers out loud. There is no text input, so please allow Microphone and Speech Recognition when asked, and test somewhere you can talk.

Without an account: choose Noting, tap Begin Session, and say a word or two for what you notice ("warmth", "tingling") every few seconds.

With an account: Exploration and Felt Sense use our hosted AI service and need sign-in. Use the demo account above (it holds credits) or Sign in with Apple. Tap Begin Session, wait for the facilitator's opening line, then answer aloud, for example "I notice some tension in my shoulders."
